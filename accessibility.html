Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Andrew Kashella is committed to making andrewkashella.com accessible to the widest possible audience, including people with disabilities. Everyone deserves clear, equal access to information about home financing.

Our standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. The Site is built with that standard in mind, including:

  • Semantic HTML structure with proper landmarks and heading order;
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images;
  • Labels programmatically associated with every form field;
  • Color contrast designed to meet AA ratios for text;
  • Full keyboard operability with visible focus indicators;
  • A responsive layout that supports zoom up to 200% and assistive technologies;
  • No content that flashes or relies on color alone to convey meaning.

Ongoing effort

Accessibility is a practice, not a checkbox. We review the Site against WCAG criteria as content is added, and we aim to remediate issues promptly when they are found.

Third-party content

Some features link to or embed third-party services (for example, the secure loan application platform and spam-prevention tools). While we choose vendors thoughtfully, we cannot guarantee the accessibility of third-party platforms.

Feedback & assistance

If you experience any difficulty accessing any part of this Site, or would like information from the Site provided in an alternative format, please contact us. We will gladly assist, including providing the information you need by phone or email:
